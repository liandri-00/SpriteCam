SpriteCam

Turns a photo into handheld-console pixel art: tiny palettes, dithering and an old LCD screen. Everything happens on your device, with no AI and no uploads.

Palette
Resolution

Pixels on the photo's short side. The Game Boy screen was 144 pixels tall.

Screen
LCD effect

The LCD effect draws the gaps between pixels, like an old handheld screen. Auto contrast stretches dim or hazy photos across the whole palette.

How to use it

  1. Take or choose a photo

    Use your camera, pick one from your gallery, or drop a file on the screen.

  2. Pick a palette

    Each palette is a different old screen. Raise the resolution if faces look too blocky.

  3. Save or share

    You get a PNG with crisp, square pixels, ready to post.

Your photos stay yours

Everything runs in your browser. Photos are never uploaded, there's no account, no analytics and no AI.
