SpriteCam

Privacy note · last updated 24 September 2026

SpriteCam collects nothing. Your photos are processed inside your browser and never leave your device.

Your photos

  • When you take or choose a photo, your browser reads it into memory on your device. The pixel-art conversion runs there too, in plain JavaScript.
  • The live camera works the same way: each frame is converted on your device and then thrown away. Nothing is recorded. The camera turns off when you take a shot, close it or leave the page.
  • Photos and results are never sent to a server. There is no server: the site is a set of static files.
  • Nothing is stored after you close the page. SpriteCam uses no cookies, no local storage and no databases.
  • When you save or share an image, it goes where you send it: your downloads folder or the app you pick in your device's share sheet.

No tracking

  • No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no AI services.
  • Fonts and icons are served from this site, so the page makes no requests to third parties.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser downloads the page, for security and operations. SpriteCam has no access to those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The support link takes you to Ko-fi. Nothing from Ko-fi is loaded on SpriteCam itself; once you follow the link, Ko-fi's own privacy policy applies.

Questions

The source code is public, so you can check all of this yourself. Questions are welcome as an issue on GitHub.
